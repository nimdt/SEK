About Us

Soul Eats Kitchen is a not-for-profit food service helping Australians who are house-rich but cash-poor, pensioners, students, and anyone struggling to put food on the table. No judgment. No paperwork. Just good, healthy food — for everyone who needs it.

“I’ve worked all my life but after an illness I couldn’t afford groceries. Soul Eats made sure I didn’t go hungry.”
